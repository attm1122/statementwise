## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data only for the purposes described below and in accordance with the principle of purpose limitation (Art. 5(1)(b) GDPR).

### 3.1 Purpose Overview

| Purpose | Description | Data Used |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| **Service Delivery** | Converting your PDF bank statements to structured formats (QBO, OFX, CSV) | Uploaded statements, account data, extracted data |
| **Account Management** | Creating and managing your account, authentication, subscription management | Identity data, technical data |
| **Customer Support** | Responding to your questions and resolving issues | All data as necessary |
| **Security and Fraud Prevention** | Protecting our platform and users from unauthorized access and fraud | Technical data, usage data |
| **Service Improvement** | Improving the accuracy of our AI extraction and overall platform experience | Anonymized usage data, error logs |
| **Billing** | Processing payments and managing subscriptions | Identity data, payment data |
| **Legal Compliance** | Complying with applicable laws and regulations | All data as necessary |
| **Communication** | Sending service notifications, updates, and marketing (with opt-out) | Identity data |

### 3.2 Detailed Purpose Descriptions

#### 3.2.1 AI Extraction and Conversion (Core Service)

Our primary processing activity is the conversion of PDF bank statements into structured accounting formats. This involves:

1. **Document parsing** — Extracting text and numerical data from the uploaded PDF
2. **AI-powered data extraction** — Using Moonshot AI's large language model to identify and extract structured transaction data
3. **Data validation** — Checking extracted data for consistency and accuracy
4. **Format conversion** — Transforming extracted data into the requested export format (QBO, OFX, or CSV)
5. **Presentation** — Displaying extracted data for your review before export

> **Important:** You have the opportunity to review and correct all extracted data before export. The AI does not make decisions about you — it performs technical data extraction only.

#### 3.2.2 Account Management

We process your account data to:
- Create and maintain your account
- Authenticate your login attempts
- Manage your subscription and billing
- Provide account recovery options
- Communicate important service information

#### 3.2.3 Customer Support

When you contact our support team, we use your data to:
- Identify your account and service history
- Investigate and resolve reported issues
- Follow up on open support tickets
- Improve our support processes

#### 3.2.4 Security and Fraud Prevention

We process technical and usage data to:
- Detect and prevent unauthorized access to accounts
- Identify suspicious upload patterns or abuse
- Investigate security incidents
- Comply with legal obligations related to financial crime prevention

#### 3.2.5 Service Improvement

We analyze anonymized usage data to:
- Improve the accuracy of our AI extraction models
- Identify and fix bugs and usability issues
- Develop new features based on user needs
- Optimize platform performance

> **Note:** We do not use your actual bank statement data to train AI models. Any model improvement uses only anonymized, aggregated usage patterns.

#### 3.2.6 Marketing Communications

With your consent or based on our legitimate interest, we may send:
- Product updates and new feature announcements

- Promotional offers for related services

| Processing Activity | Legal Basis | GDPR Article | Explanation |

| Billing and payment processing | Performance of a contract | Art. 6(1)(b) | Processing payment data is necessary to fulfill our subscription contract. |

| Service improvement and analytics | Legitimate interests | Art. 6(1)(f) | We have a legitimate interest in improving our service quality and user experience. |

| Marketing communications to non-customers | Consent | Art. 6(1)(a) | We will only send marketing communications to non-customers with their explicit consent. |
| Cookie consent management | Legal obligation | Art. 6(1)(c) | Recording cookie consent preferences is required to demonstrate compliance. |

| Legitimate Interest | Our Interest | Your Rights Impact | Balancing Result |

Our full Legitimate Interests Assessment is available upon request from our DPO.

### 7.2 Detailed Rights Descriptions

#### 7.2.1 Right to Access (Art. 15)

You have the right to obtain from us:
- Confirmation as to whether we process your personal data
- Access to your personal data
- Information about the processing purposes, categories of data, recipients, retention periods, and your other rights

**How to request:** Email dpo@statementwise.ai with subject line "Data Access Request" and attach proof of identity (government-issued photo ID).

#### 7.2.2 Right to Rectification (Art. 16)

You have the right to have inaccurate personal data corrected and incomplete data completed.

**How to request:**
- Account information: Update directly in your account settings
- Other data: Email dpo@statementwise.ai with details of the correction required

#### 7.2.3 Right to Erasure (Art. 17)

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data where:
- The data is no longer necessary for the purposes collected
- You withdraw consent and no other legal basis applies
- You object to processing and we have no overriding legitimate grounds
- The data has been unlawfully processed
- Erasure is required by law

**How to request:** Email dpo@statementwise.ai with subject line "Erasure Request."

**Exceptions:** We may retain data where required for legal obligations, legal claims, or archiving purposes.

#### 7.2.4 Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18)

You have the right to request restriction of processing where:
- You contest the accuracy of the data
- The processing is unlawful and you oppose erasure
- We no longer need the data but you require it for legal claims
- You have objected to processing pending verification

**How to request:** Email dpo@statementwise.ai with subject line "Restriction Request."

#### 7.2.5 Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller.

**How to request:** Email dpo@statementwise.ai with subject line "Portability Request" and specify your preferred format (JSON, CSV).

#### 7.2.6 Right to Object (Art. 21)

You have the right to object to:
- Processing based on legitimate interests (including profiling)
- Processing for direct marketing purposes

**How to request:**
- Marketing: Click "Unsubscribe" in any email or email dpo@statementwise.ai
- Other processing: Email dpo@statementwise.ai with your specific objection and grounds

#### 7.2.7 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making (Art. 22)

Statementwise does **not** make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Our AI performs **technical data extraction** — it does not evaluate, score, or make decisions about individuals.

If we introduce automated decision-making in the future, we will inform you and provide the right to human intervention, express your point of view, and contest the decision.

### 7.3 Identity Verification

To protect your privacy, we may need to verify your identity before processing your request. We will ask for:
- Government-issued photo ID
- Proof of account ownership (e.g., access to your registered email address)

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

### 7.4 Response Timeline

We respond to all requests within **30 calendar days**. For complex requests, we may extend this period by up to two months, notifying you within the initial 30-day period of the extension and reasons.

### 7.5 Fees

We do not charge fees for exercising your rights, unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive (e.g., repetitive requests). In such cases, we may charge a reasonable fee or refuse to act on the request.

We primarily process personal data within the **European Economic Area (EEA)**. However, certain sub-processors are located outside the EEA. When we transfer your personal data outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

### 9.2 Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

For transfers to countries without an adequacy decision, we use the **EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914)**. These are contractual commitments between the data exporter and importer that ensure an equivalent level of data protection.

### 9.3 Transfer Impact Assessment

In accordance with the Schrems II judgment and EDPB recommendations, we have conducted a Transfer Impact Assessment for data transfers to China. This assessment evaluates the laws and practices of the destination country and the supplementary measures applied.

Our Transfer Impact Assessment is available upon request from our DPO.

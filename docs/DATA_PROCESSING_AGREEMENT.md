## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

In this DPA, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below. Capitalized terms not defined herein shall have the meanings given to them in the Statementwise Terms of Service or Subscription Agreement (the "**Main Agreement**").

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|

| "**Controller**" | The Accounting Firm, as defined in the Main Agreement, who determines the purposes and means of the processing of Personal Data. |
| "**Data Subject**" | An identified or identifiable natural person to whom the Personal Data relates. |
| "**GDPR**" | Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data. |

## 2. Scope and Purpose of Processing

### 2.1 Subject Matter

The subject matter of this DPA is the Processing of Personal Data contained in PDF bank statements uploaded by or on behalf of the Controller to the Statementwise platform for the purpose of conversion to structured accounting formats.

### 2.2 Duration

The Processing will continue for the duration of the Main Agreement, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 13.

### 2.3 Nature and Purpose of Processing

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Nature of Processing** | Automated and manual data extraction, transformation, storage, and export of financial data from PDF bank statements |
| **Purpose of Processing** | Converting PDF bank statements to structured accounting formats (QBO, OFX, CSV) for the Controller's accounting and bookkeeping purposes |
| **Categories of Data Subjects** | The Controller's clients (natural persons) whose bank statements are uploaded |
| **Categories of Personal Data** | Account holder names; bank account numbers (full/partial); transaction details (dates, descriptions, amounts, counterparty information); balance information; statement metadata |
| **Special Category Data** | Statementwise does not intentionally process special category data under Art. 9 GDPR. However, bank statements may inadvertently contain data that could reveal sensitive information. |

### 2.4 Controller's Processing Instructions

The Controller instructs the Processor to process Personal Data only for the following purposes:

1. **Core Conversion Service** — Extracting structured transaction data from uploaded PDF bank statements and converting it to the Controller's selected export format (QBO, OFX, or CSV)
2. **Data Storage** — Storing converted data within the Controller's client portal for the duration of the subscription term
3. **Data Access** — Making converted data available to authorized users designated by the Controller
4. **Data Export** — Enabling the Controller to export converted data to their own systems
5. **Technical Support** — Using data as necessary to provide customer support to the Controller
6. **Security and Compliance** — Processing as necessary to maintain platform security and comply with applicable law

The Processor shall **not** process Personal Data for any other purpose without the Controller's prior written authorization.

### 2.5 Lawful Basis

The Controller warrants that:

1. It has a valid lawful basis under Art. 6 GDPR for processing the Personal Data and sharing it with the Processor
2. Where the Controller's clients are data subjects, it has provided appropriate privacy information as required by Art. 13-14 GDPR
3. It has obtained any necessary consents or authorizations from Data Subjects
4. The Processing does not violate any applicable law, regulation, or third-party right

## 3. Statementwise's Obligations as Processor

### 3.1 Processing Only on Documented Instructions (Art. 28(3)(a))

Statementwise shall:

1. Process Personal Data **only** on documented instructions from the Controller, including the instructions set out in this DPA
2. Not process Personal Data for purposes other than those authorized by the Controller
3. Immediately inform the Controller if, in Statementwise's opinion, an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law
4. Not use Personal Data for Statementwise's own commercial purposes, including without limitation for advertising, profiling, or building user profiles

### 3.2 Confidentiality (Art. 28(3)(b))

Statementwise shall ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data:

1. Are subject to a duty of confidentiality, whether contractual or statutory
2. Have received appropriate data protection training
3. Are granted access to Personal Data on a strict need-to-know basis

### 3.3 Security Measures (Art. 28(3)(c) and Art. 32)

Statementwise shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk. See [Section 6](#6-security-measures) for details.

### 3.4 Sub-processors (Art. 28(2) and 28(4))

Statementwise shall:

1. Not engage Sub-processors without the Controller's prior written authorization
2. Provide the Controller with a current list of authorized Sub-processors (see [Section 5](#5-sub-processors))
3. Ensure that Sub-processors are bound by data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA
4. Remain fully liable to the Controller for the performance of Sub-processors' obligations

### 3.5 Data Subject Rights (Art. 28(3)(e))

Statementwise shall, taking into account the nature of the Processing:

1. Assist the Controller in responding to requests from Data Subjects to exercise their rights under Chapter III GDPR (Art. 15-22)
2. Facilitate the Controller's compliance with its obligations under Art. 32-36 GDPR
3. Provide the Controller with the information necessary to demonstrate compliance

### 3.6 Assistance with Compliance (Art. 28(3)(f) and 28(3)(g))

Statementwise shall assist the Controller in:

1. Ensuring compliance with security obligations under Art. 32 GDPR
2. Notifying Personal Data Breaches to Supervisory Authorities under Art. 33 GDPR
3. Communicating with Data Subjects about breaches under Art. 34 GDPR
4. Conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments under Art. 35 GDPR
5. Consulting with Supervisory Authorities where required under Art. 36 GDPR

### 3.7 Return and Deletion of Data (Art. 28(3)(g))

At the end of the provision of services, Statementwise shall, at the Controller's choice:

1. Return all Personal Data to the Controller; or
2. Delete all Personal Data (subject to applicable legal retention requirements)

See [Section 9](#9-data-return-and-deletion) for details.

### 3.8 Audit and Inspection (Art. 28(3)(h))

Statementwise shall make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations laid down in Art. 28 GDPR and allow for and contribute to audits and inspections. See [Section 10](#10-audit-rights) for details.

### 3.9 Records of Processing (Art. 30(2))

Statementwise shall maintain a record of all categories of Processing activities carried out on behalf of the Controller, in accordance with Art. 30(2) GDPR.

## 4. Controller's Obligations

### 4.1 Lawful Basis and Transparency

The Controller shall:

1. Ensure that a valid lawful basis exists for Processing the Personal Data and sharing it with Statementwise
2. Provide Data Subjects with appropriate privacy information as required by Art. 13-14 GDPR, including disclosure of Statementwise's role as Processor

1. Maintain appropriate security measures to protect its account credentials
2. Ensure that only authorized personnel have access to the client portal
3. Immediately notify Statementwise of any suspected unauthorized access
4. Implement appropriate access controls within its organization

### 4.5 Instructions

The Controller shall:

1. Provide clear and documented instructions for Processing Personal Data
2. Not instruct Statementwise to process Personal Data in a manner that would violate Applicable Data Protection Law
3. Notify Statementwise promptly of any changes to Processing instructions

### 4.6 Data Subject Communications

The Controller is responsible for:

1. Responding to Data Subject rights requests as the primary point of contact
2. Communicating with Data Subjects regarding any Personal Data Breach
3. Maintaining records of Data Subject communications

## 6. Security Measures

### 6.1 Technical and Organizational Measures

Statementwise shall implement the following technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data:

| Domain | Measure | Detail |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Encryption at Rest** | AES-256 | All stored Personal Data, including bank statements, transaction data, and backups |
| **Encryption in Transit** | TLS 1.3 | All data transmission between systems and to/from users |
| **Key Management** | Cloud KMS with HSM | Encryption keys stored separately from data; regular rotation |
| **Access Control** | RBAC | Role-based access control; principle of least privilege |
| **Authentication** | MFA / Passwordless | Multi-factor authentication; magic link authentication |
| **Session Security** | Short-lived tokens | 15-minute JWT expiry; secure httpOnly cookies |
| **Network Security** | WAF, DDoS, VPC | Web Application Firewall; DDoS protection; private subnets |
| **Audit Logging** | Comprehensive logs | All access and modification events logged; 1-year retention |
| **Data Minimization** | Purpose-limited | Only data necessary for conversion is collected and processed |
| **Pseudonymization** | Tokenization | Internal identifiers are pseudonymized |
| **Vulnerability Management** | Regular testing | Weekly automated scans; quarterly penetration testing |
| **Incident Response** | 24/7 capability | Documented incident response plan; trained response team |

### 6.2 Additional Measures for Client Portals

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|---------------|
| **Firm isolation** | Each firm's client data is stored in a logically separated environment |
| **User-level access control** | Firm admins can define which users can access which client data |
| **Activity logging** | All access to client data is logged and auditable by the firm |
| **Data export control** | Export activities are logged and subject to audit |

### 6.3 Security Reviews

Statementwise shall:

1. Conduct annual security risk assessments
2. Perform regular penetration testing (at least annually)
3. Maintain vulnerability management with regular patching
4. Review and update security measures as technology evolves

### 6.4 Security Incidents

Statementwise shall notify the Controller of any Security Incident affecting Personal Data without undue delay and no later than **24 hours** after becoming aware of the incident. See [Section 8](#8-personal-data-breach-notification).

## 7. Data Subject Rights

### 7.1 Cooperation on Data Subject Requests

Statementwise shall assist the Controller in responding to Data Subject rights requests under Chapter III GDPR. The Parties shall cooperate as follows:

| Right (GDPR Article) | Controller's Responsibility | Processor's Responsibility |
|---------------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Right to Access** (Art. 15) | Primary responsibility to respond; verify Data Subject identity | Provide access to Personal Data held; extract and format data for Controller |
| **Right to Rectification** (Art. 16) | Verify accuracy and instruct Processor | Implement corrections as instructed by Controller |
| **Right to Erasure** (Art. 17) | Assess applicability and instruct Processor | Delete specified data within 72 hours of instruction |
| **Right to Restriction** (Art. 18) | Assess applicability and instruct Processor | Restrict processing as instructed |
| **Right to Portability** (Art. 20) | Primary responsibility to respond | Provide data in structured, machine-readable format (CSV, OFX, QBO) |
| **Right to Object** (Art. 21) | Assess objection and instruct Processor | Cease processing as instructed |

### 7.2 Request Handling Procedure

1. **Controller receives request** — The Controller is the primary point of contact for Data Subjects
2. **Controller forwards to Statementwise** — If Processor assistance is required, the Controller forwards the request with necessary context
3. **Statementwise responds** — Statementwise provides the requested data or action within **10 business days**
4. **Controller responds to Data Subject** — The Controller compiles and delivers the final response

### 7.3 Identity Verification

The Controller is responsible for verifying the identity of Data Subjects making rights requests. Statementwise shall verify that requests received directly from Data Subjects are forwarded by the Controller.

## 8. Personal Data Breach Notification

### 8.1 Breach Notification Timeline

Statementwise shall notify the Controller of any Personal Data Breach **without undue delay** and in any case within **24 hours** of becoming aware of the breach.

### 8.2 Breach Notification Content

The notification shall include, to the extent available:

| Information Element | Description |
|-------------------|-------------|
| **Nature of breach** | Type of breach (confidentiality, integrity, availability); categories and approximate number of affected Data Subjects and records |
| **Likely consequences** | Description of the likely consequences for Data Subjects |
| **Measures taken** | Description of measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate potential harm |
| **Contact details** | Name and contact details of the Statementwise representative handling the breach |
| **Additional information** | Any other information that may assist the Controller in complying with its obligations under Art. 33-34 GDPR |

### 8.3 Cooperation on Breach Response

Statementwise shall:

1. Provide reasonable assistance to the Controller in investigating the breach
2. Cooperate with the Controller in preparing breach notifications to Supervisory Authorities and Data Subjects
3. Take appropriate measures to contain and remediate the breach
4. Provide regular updates on the status of the breach investigation

### 8.4 Documentation

Statementwise shall document all Personal Data Breaches, including:
- Facts relating to the breach
- Effects of the breach
- Remedial action taken
- Documentation shall be made available to the Controller upon request

### 8.5 Controller's Responsibilities

Following notification, the Controller is responsible for:

1. Assessing whether the breach is likely to result in a risk to Data Subjects' rights and freedoms
2. Notifying the relevant Supervisory Authority within 72 hours where required by Art. 33
3. Notifying affected Data Subjects without undue delay where required by Art. 34
4. Maintaining records of all Personal Data Breaches

## 9. Data Return and Deletion

### 9.1 Return or Deletion on Termination

Upon termination or expiry of the Main Agreement (or any earlier date requested by the Controller), Statementwise shall, at the Controller's election:

**Option A — Data Return:**
- Export all Personal Data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (CSV, OFX, or QBO)
- Make the export available for download within **10 business days**
- The Controller may download the exported data within **30 days**

**Option B — Data Deletion:**
- Delete all Personal Data within **30 days** of termination
- Provide a deletion certificate confirming deletion
- Retain only anonymized data for statistical purposes where permitted by law

### 9.2 Retention Obligations

Notwithstanding the above, Statementwise may retain Personal Data where required by:

1. **Applicable law** — Where retention is required by EU or Member State law
2. **Legal claims** — Where necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims
3. **Audit requirements** — Where required for regulatory or compliance audits

In such cases, Statementwise shall:
- Inform the Controller of the legal basis for retention
- Restrict processing to the specific purpose of retention
- Delete the data as soon as the retention basis no longer applies

### 9.3 Backup Data

Personal Data may remain in encrypted backups for up to **90 days** following deletion due to backup rotation cycles. Such data:
- Is encrypted with AES-256
- Is not accessible for operational purposes
- Is overwritten in the normal course of backup rotation

### 9.4 Ongoing Deletion Obligations

Statementwise shall delete Personal Data in accordance with the retention periods set out in the Privacy Policy, even during the term of the Main Agreement.

The Controller has the right to audit Statementwise's compliance with this DPA, subject to the conditions set out in this Section.

### 10.2 Audit Procedure

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|

| **2. Notice** | At least **30 days** advance notice required |
| **3. Frequency** | Maximum **once per calendar year** (unless additional audits are required by Supervisory Authority) |
| **4. Scope** | Limited to verification of compliance with this DPA |

2. The Controller shall bear the cost of the audit, unless material non-compliance is identified

4. The audit shall comply with applicable security policies

### 10.4 Alternative Evidence

The Controller agrees that Statementwise's current security certifications and audit reports (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2) shall satisfy the audit obligation, provided:
1. The certification/report covers the relevant processing activities
2. The certification/report is dated within the last 12 months
3. Statementwise makes the full report available to the Controller (under NDA if required)

### 10.5 Supervisory Authority Audits

Where a Supervisory Authority requests access to Statementwise's facilities, Statementwise shall:
1. Notify the Controller without undue delay
2. Cooperate with the Supervisory Authority
3. Provide the Controller with a copy of any findings relevant to the Controller's processing

## 12. Liability and Indemnification

### 12.1 Statementwise Liability

Statementwise shall be liable for damages caused by Processing that violates the GDPR or this DPA, except where Statementwise is not responsible for the event giving rise to the damage.

### 12.2 Controller Liability

The Controller shall be liable for damages caused by:
1. Uploading Personal Data without a valid lawful basis
2. Providing inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data
3. Breaching its obligations under this DPA
4. Instructing Statementwise to process Personal Data in violation of Applicable Data Protection Law

### 12.3 Indemnification

Each Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other Party from and against any claims, damages, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from:
1. The indemnifying Party's breach of this DPA
2. The indemnifying Party's violation of Applicable Data Protection Law

### 12.4 Limitation of Liability

Nothing in this DPA limits or excludes either Party's liability for:
1. Death or personal injury caused by negligence
2. Fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation
3. Any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by applicable law

### 12.5 GDPR Liability (Art. 82)

The Parties acknowledge that:
1. Each Party is liable for the damage caused by Processing only where it has not complied with obligations of the GDPR specifically directed to Processors or Controllers
2. A Controller or Processor shall be exempt from liability if it proves that it is not in any way responsible for the event giving rise to the damage
3. Where more than one Controller or Processor is involved in the same Processing, each shall be jointly and severally liable for the entire amount of the damage
